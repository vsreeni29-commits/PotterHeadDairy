A forgotten diary on a lamplit writing desk. Dark-only surroundings, warm parchment as the one luminous surface, real black-brown ink, antique brass used sparingly. Generous pacing on the page, compact quiet navigation. No Hogwarts clipart, gradients in text, neon, floating magical trinkets or gamification. Cormorant Garamond for literary headings, Inter for small functional controls, Caveat for readable handwritten ink. Thin engraved rules, subtly imperfect paper edges and book binding supply the atmosphere. The writing itself is the spectacle: completed sentences diffuse into paper; reflections gather out of it. Reduced motion makes both immediate. Mobile is a single uninterrupted sheet; controls remain comfortable and the keyboard must never cover the writing. All text stays readable; mystery never hides save status or privacy facts.
